# Datasets Registry: Pond Health Alert (PS 1.1)

All datasets used in Pond Health Alert are documented here with source, licence, provenance, purpose, and known limitations.

---

## 1. Aquaculture Water-Quality Dataset
- **Status / Label:** Synthetic / Derived Rules
- **Samples:** ~4,300 samples
- **Parameters:** Temperature, pH, Dissolved Oxygen, Turbidity, Conductivity, Ammonia
- **Source:** Kaggle / Public aquaculture water quality measurements repository
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0 / Open Access
- **Limitations & Transparency:**
  > [!NOTE]
  > The alert and hazard labels in this dataset were generated by applying heuristic thresholds. The trained secondary ML model therefore largely learns these rule bounds rather than novel bio-chemical correlations.

---

## 2. Montería Tilapia Pond IoT Data
- **Status / Label:** Real Sensor Readings
- **Source:** University of Córdoba aquaculture telemetry sensor station (Montería, Colombia)
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- **Purpose:** Used in the **Replay Simulator** to stream realistic diurnal water temperature, dissolved oxygen fluctuations, and pH curves with diurnal sensor noise.
- **Limitations:** Monitored in warm tropical climate ponds for Tilapia; coastal Karnataka saline intrusion effects require calibrated ranges.

---

## 3. AquaSense Synthetic Stress Dataset
- **Status / Label:** Synthetic (Explicitly labelled in UI)
- **Source:** Internally generated telemetry replay dataset with calibrated fault injection.
- **Purpose:** Specifically engineered for stress-testing alert logic:
  - Sensor dropouts and missing telemetry packets (triggers `CANT_TELL`).
  - Stale data (> 48 hours without update).
  - Rapid dissolved oxygen hypoxia crash (< 3.0 mg/L) with concurrent fish mortality.
  - Sensor drift and stuck transducer values.

---

## 4. Freshwater Fish Disease Dataset (South Asia)
- **Status / Label:** Real Field Photos
- **Source:** Open-access Asian aquaculture research repository (Bangladesh & Indian sub-continent carp & catfish pond surveys)
- **Licence:** Research Open Access / CC BY 4.0
- **Classes:** 7 classes:
  1. `bacterial_red_spot` (Epizootic Ulcerative Syndrome / Aeromonas)
  2. `bacterial_gill_rot`
  3. `fin_tail_rot`
  4. `dropsy`
  5. `fungal_infection` (Saprolegniasis)
  6. `parasitic_white_spot` (Ichthyophthiriasis)
  7. `healthy`
- **Limitations:** Highly variable field lighting, manual mobile phone captures. Requires the Photo Quality Gate (blur, exposure, framing) to prevent misdiagnosis.

---

## 5. MatsyaDx-BD & ShrimpDiseaseImageBD
- **Status / Label:** Real Field Photos (Supplementary)
- **Source:** MatsyaDx carp and freshwater prawn clinical collection
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0
- **Purpose:** Evaluation benchmarks for out-of-distribution robustness.
